Study Method

The Feynman Technique

Explain concepts in simple terms as if teaching a beginner. If you get stuck, go back and study the gap.

Spaced Repetition

Review concepts at increasing intervals: day 1, day 3, day 7, day 14, day 30.

Hands-On First

Always pair theory with practice. Deploy, break, fix, repeat.

Mock Interviews

Record yourself answering "walk me through" questions. Review and improve.
